Section 01

Overview & Data Collection Philosophy

Muhammad LLC ("we," "our," or "us") operates the StrictAlarm mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how StrictAlarm handles information when you use the App on Android.

StrictAlarm is designed to function as an on-device alarm and wake-up mission utility. We do not operate external backend servers to collect, sell, rent, or monetize your personal data. Your alarms, wake-up mission preferences, and app settings are stored locally on your device.

Section 02

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Depending on how you configure and use StrictAlarm, the following information is stored locally on your device (via local database and preferences storage):

Alarm & Schedule Data
Alarm times, labels/names, memos, days of the week, one-time dates, sound/ringtone selections, volume and vibration settings, wake-up check settings, and alarm state.
Wake-Up Mission Configurations & Custom Content
Mission settings (such as math difficulty/question counts, trivia preferences, shake counts, custom typing sentences, and barcode/QR mission items or reference images you configure).
App Preferences
Display settings (such as 12-hour/24-hour time format), sound effect toggles, mission time limits, and onboarding/permission states.
Section 03

Permissions & How They Are Used

StrictAlarm requests specific Android permissions solely to deliver alarm reliability and optional wake-up missions:

Exact alarms & wake locks

SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM USE_EXACT_ALARM WAKE_LOCK

Used to trigger alarms accurately at scheduled times and keep the device/CPU awake long enough to ring and display wake-up screens.

Audio & vibration

VIBRATE alarm audio playback

Used to play selected alarm ringtones, optional mission sound effects, and vibration patterns.

Notifications, full-screen & overlay

POST_NOTIFICATIONS SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT

Used to alert you when an alarm triggers and present the wake-up screen even when the device is locked or another app is in the foreground.

Battery optimization & boot

REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

Used to help prevent the operating system from suppressing scheduled alarms and to reschedule alarms after a device reboot.

Sensors (accelerometer / motion)

Motion sensor

Used during Shake missions to detect physical movement of your device. Sensor readings are processed in real time in memory to count shakes and are not stored or transmitted.

Camera

android.permission.CAMERA

Used only if you choose camera-based missions such as Barcode/QR Scanning or Face Movement verification.

On-device processing. Camera frames and face movement/barcode detection are processed locally on your device. Camera video, photos, or facial geometry/biometric data are not uploaded to any server or shared with third parties.

Section 05

Data Retention & Deletion

All app data resides locally on your device. You can edit or delete individual alarms and configurations inside the App at any time, or remove all local app data by clearing the App's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling StrictAlarm.

Section 06
